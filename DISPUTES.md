# Disputes

For a dispute over a package name, or a legal takedown request. Report a
security problem through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md) instead.

## How to raise one

Open an issue in this repository, or for a legal request write to the operator,
Roman Zaiev, at dev@hyphatech.io. Say which name or version it concerns and what
you are asking for, with your evidence.

Evidence that counts: control of the upstream repository, official opam's
metadata, prior publication, and trademark.

## Outcomes

- No action.
- Revoke the versions concerned, with a public record.
- Transfer the name, by changing its owners record and revoking the old
  versions.

A transferred name starts at a new version. The old version numbers are not
freed, because a published version is never replaced.

A name that appears in official opam from another repository is settled without
a dispute: official opam wins, and the versions here are revoked.

## Timing

The targets are acknowledgement within three business days and a decision within
fourteen.
