# Governance

open-opam is a community opam repository. It is complementary to official
opam, not affiliated with `ocaml/opam-repository`.

## Maintainers

- Roman Zaiev ([zaiev](https://github.com/zaiev)), operator

Maintainers hold a hardware key on their GitHub account. Any maintainer can
trigger a revocation for a security, legal or author's reason. A contributor
who has been active in the registry can ask to join by opening an issue, and
the maintainers decide.

## How changes land

A publication lands through the registry's automated checks, with no human
approval. A change to the registry's own code, workflows or documents is a
maintainer's pull request.

## Conflict of interest

A maintainer who decides a dispute or a revocation over their own package, or
one of their employer's, says so on the issue and publishes the reasoning.
